Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

AmbiVision ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you visit our website or engage with our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact information: name, email address, company name, phone number — provided voluntarily through our inquiry form.
  • Project information: service requirements, budget, timeline, and project description submitted via our contact form.
  • Usage data: basic analytics data including page views and session duration (collected anonymously via privacy-respecting analytics, if configured).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information exclusively to:

  • Respond to your project inquiry and communicate about our services.
  • Provide quotes, proposals, and relevant project documentation.
  • Maintain records of business communications for legal and operational purposes.

We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing, and we do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Data Retention

We retain inquiry data for up to 24 months. If you request deletion of your information, we will comply within 30 days, subject to any legal obligations to retain records.

4. Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss. Form submissions are encrypted in transit via HTTPS.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as:

  • Google Fonts — for typography (subject to Google's privacy policy).
  • A form processing service (when configured) — for handling contact form submissions.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal data.
  • Withdraw consent for any optional data use at any time.

To exercise these rights, please email us at hello@ambivision.studio.

7. Cookies

Our website does not currently use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. If analytics are enabled, only privacy-respecting, anonymised session data is collected.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of our website constitutes acceptance of any updates.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at hello@ambivision.studio.
